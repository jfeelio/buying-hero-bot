# Buying Hero SEO Audit — 2026-03-09

---

## Executive Summary

Buying Hero's website has a solid structural foundation — all crawled pages carry valid schema markup, PageSpeed desktop scores are generally strong (91–97 on most key pages), and the Carrot platform provides reliable canonical signals. However, the site has several compounding SEO issues that are actively suppressing organic lead flow. The most urgent problem is a three-way homepage duplicate caused by http://www.buyinghero.com, https://buyinghero.com, and https://www.buyinghero.com all rendering the same title, description, and content without a proper 301-redirect chain consolidating equity to a single canonical URL. This dilutes ranking authority for the site's most important page and confuses Google's crawl budget.

Beyond the canonicalization issue, five high-value pages are missing H1 tags (including the 'How We Buy Houses' process page), multiple key pages carry titles under 50 characters that waste available SERP real estate, and the mobile LCP on critical conversion pages like the homepage (5.8 s) and the 'Our Company' page (12.2 s) far exceed Google's 2.5 s 'Good' threshold — a direct ranking factor and a conversion killer for motivated sellers searching on smartphones in distress. The site also has zero location pages for the primary target markets of Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties beyond Miami itself, meaning there is no organic footprint for high-intent queries like 'sell my house fast Fort Lauderdale' or 'cash home buyers Boca Raton.'

Immediate priorities in order of lead-generation impact are: (1) enforce HTTPS + www or non-www redirect consolidation, (2) fix all missing H1 tags on money pages, (3) rewrite all meta titles and descriptions to hit character targets and include location + intent keywords, (4) aggressively optimize mobile LCP site-wide, and (5) build out dedicated location landing pages for South Florida's top cash-buyer markets. Addressing these items within 30–60 days is projected to materially improve crawl efficiency, CTR from SERPs, and mobile conversion rates.

## Critical Issues

1. 1. HOMEPAGE URL CANONICALIZATION / REDIRECT FAILURE: http://www.buyinghero.com, https://buyinghero.com, and https://www.buyinghero.com all load as separate crawlable URLs with identical content — PageRank and link equity is split across three versions. A permanent 301 redirect must consolidate all variants to a single preferred URL (https://www.buyinghero.com or https://buyinghero.com) immediately.
2. 2. MISSING H1 ON 'HOW WE BUY HOUSES': The site's primary process/trust page has no H1 tag, making it invisible to Google for process-related queries and weakening the page's topical relevance signal for 'sell my house fast Miami' variants.
3. 3. MOBILE LCP EXCEEDS 5 SECONDS ON ALL KEY CONVERSION PAGES: Homepage (5.8 s), Get a Cash Offer (5.3 s), How We Buy Houses (5.1 s), Contact Us (5.1 s) all fail Core Web Vitals LCP benchmarks on mobile — Google uses mobile-first indexing and this directly suppresses rankings and increases bounce rates among distressed sellers on phones.
4. 4. 'OUR COMPANY' PAGE MOBILE PERFORMANCE CRITICAL: LCP of 12.2 s on mobile with a performance score of 56 is severe — this page likely contains an unoptimized large image (headshot 803x1024px) that must be compressed, resized, and served in next-gen format (WebP/AVIF).
5. 5. DUPLICATE META TITLES AND DESCRIPTIONS ACROSS THREE HOMEPAGE VARIANTS: The same title 'Sell Your House Fast in Miami, FL | Cash Offers' and same description appear on three distinct crawlable URLs, triggering Google duplicate content signals and splitting CTR authority.
6. 6. MISSING H1 TAGS ON /terms-conditions, /go/learn-the-pros-and-cons..., AND /go/free-guide-5-ways...: While two of these are noindexed (intentionally), /terms-conditions is indexed and lacks an H1 — a minor but unnecessary signal gap.
7. 7. MISSING CANONICAL TAG ON /privacy AND /resource-page: Both pages lack a canonical tag entirely; /resource-page also has a noindex directive and an SEO score of 66 — these pages need canonical tags added regardless of index status to prevent equity leakage.
8. 8. ZERO SOUTH FLORIDA LOCATION PAGES BEYOND MIAMI: The site has location pages for Clermont and Winter Garden (Central Florida) but none for Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Hialeah, Hollywood, Pompano Beach, or other high-value South Florida markets — the stated primary service area has no organic SEO coverage.
9. 9. BLOG INDEX PAGE IS THIN AND GENERIC: /blog has only 220 words, an 18-character meta title ('Blog | Buying Hero'), and a near-empty meta description ('Latest posts: Blog…') — the blog is a critical content channel for long-tail foreclosure, probate, and distressed-seller queries and is currently contributing nothing to organic rankings.
10. 10. REVIEWS PAGE THIN CONTENT (333 WORDS) WITH OVERSIZED META DESCRIPTION: Social proof pages are high-conversion trust assets; at 333 words the page is below the 500-word threshold and its 204-character meta description will be truncated in SERPs, wasting the trust signal opportunity in the click path.

## Priority Fixes

### HTTP non-www variant not redirecting to canonical HTTPS www (or non-www) URL
**Page:** http://www.buyinghero.com
**Current:** http://www.buyinghero.com loads as a separate crawlable page with duplicate content — no redirect in place
**Recommended:** 301 permanent redirect http://www.buyinghero.com → https://www.buyinghero.com (or https://buyinghero.com if non-www is preferred canonical)
**Notes:** This is a server/hosting-level redirect. In Carrot, go to Settings → Site Settings → confirm your preferred domain. Then add redirect rules in your hosting control panel or Cloudflare (if used) to 301-redirect both the http:// and the non-preferred www/non-www variant to the single canonical root. Verify with a redirect checker after deployment. Also submit the canonical URL to Google Search Console as the preferred property.

### www variant and non-www variant (https://buyinghero.com) both crawlable with identical content
**Page:** https://www.buyinghero.com

**Notes:** In Carrot dashboard go to Settings → General → Site URL and set your preferred domain. Ensure canonical tags auto-generated by Carrot all use the same root. Confirm in Google Search Console under Settings → Change of Address if switching primary property.
